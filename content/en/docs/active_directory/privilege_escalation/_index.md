Privilege escalation within the Active Directory domain is usually a gradual and cyclical process of leveraging access to domain's machines and services to obtain more access until domain compromise is achieved. Due to the sheer number of services available on enterprise domains, the attack surface is massive, and new techniques are always being discovered and researched.

Successful privilege escalation within an AD environment hinges on thorough enumeration of the domain from the perspective of a domain account and thoughtful analysis of the information collected.
