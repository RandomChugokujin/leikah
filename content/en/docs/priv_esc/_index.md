After getting a shell on a target system, the next goal is to escalate our privileges and gain access as more privileged users until we get root or SYSTEM. This can involve taking advantage of misconfigured privileges and scheduled tasks, hijacking vulnerable libraries, exploiting vulnerable services running as another user, and much more.

Successful privilege escalation, much like the initial foothold, requires thorough enumeration of the operating system, its users, permissions, services, and available files.
